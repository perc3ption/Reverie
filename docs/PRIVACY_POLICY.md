# Privacy Policy for Reverie

**Last Updated:** July 2026

Welcome to Reverie. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Reverie handles information when you use the app.

By using Reverie, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. What Reverie Does

Reverie is a **local, offline music player** for curated collections. You can import music from your device, organize your library and playlists, play audio in the background, and optionally unlock Premium features through a one-time in-app purchase.

Reverie’s design principles:

- **No accounts**
- **No cloud sync**
- **No ads**
- **Your music stays on your device**

---

## 2. Information We Collect

**Reverie does not collect personal information from you.**

There is no Reverie account system, and Reverie does not upload your music, playlists, listening history, or settings to our servers. We do not operate a cloud backend for this app.

### Data that stays on your device

To make the app work, Reverie may store information **only on your device**, such as:

- Music files you choose to import
- Library metadata (for example, title, artist, album, artwork)
- Playlists, tags, ratings, and play history
- App preferences (for example, display name, theme, audio settings)

This on-device data is used solely to run Reverie on your phone. It is not sent to Perceptiveus Studios.

### Optional display name

You may set a local display name in Settings. This is stored only on your device and is not an account.

### Purchases (if you buy Premium)

If you choose to purchase Premium, **Google Play** processes the payment. Google may handle purchase-related information according to Google’s own policies. Reverie does not receive or store your payment card details.

---

## 3. How Information Is Used

On-device data is used only to:

- Play and organize your music library
- Provide playlists, search, queue, and playback features
- Remember your preferences
- Unlock or restore Premium through Google Play Billing (if purchased)

We do not sell your information. We do not use your music library for advertising or analytics tracking.

---

## 4. Purchases

Reverie may offer a free experience and an optional **one-time Premium unlock**.

Purchases are processed securely through:

- **Google Play Billing**

Reverie does not directly process or store payment information. Purchase ownership is managed by Google Play.

---

## 5. Data Storage

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Your device** | Music you import, library data, playlists, preferences, optional lyrics/artwork |
| **Google Play** | Premium purchase entitlement (only if you buy or restore Premium) |

Reverie does **not** store your library or personal data on Perceptiveus Studios servers. There is no cloud account or cloud sync for Reverie.

---

## 6. Permissions

Reverie may request permissions including:

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Notifications** | Media playback controls while music is playing |
| **Internet / network** | Google Play Billing only (purchases and restore) — not for uploading your music |
| **Audio settings** | Playback and optional audio features |
| **Foreground media playback** | Continue playing music when the app is in the background |

Reverie uses the system file picker so you can choose which music files or folders to import. Reverie does not request broad access to all files on your device.

Permissions are used only for the features they support. You may revoke permissions at any time through your device settings.

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

Reverie may use:

- **Google Play Services**
- **Google Play Billing**

These are used for optional Premium purchases. Reverie does not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or cloud databases to collect or sync your personal data.

For Google’s privacy practices, see:  
[https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

## 8. Data Security

Because your library lives on your device, protecting access to your device is the primary way your music and preferences stay private.

For purchases, Google Play uses industry-standard protections. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy

Reverie is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.
